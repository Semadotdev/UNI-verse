## Getting Started

Create an account to unlock the full experience — saving manga, commenting, posting, and earning rewards.

1. Open **UNI-verse** and tap **Register**.
2. Enter your email and a password, then confirm your account.
3. Sign in whenever you return. Your library, history, and rewards follow your account on any device.

**Install as an app (PWA).** UNI-verse works as a mobile app:

- **Mobile:** use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" / "Install app" option.
- **Desktop:** click the install icon in the address bar.

Once installed, the app launches fullscreen, loads faster, and can be opened offline.

## Finding and Reading

### Search

Use the **search** bar to find any series. Results update as you type, across all connected sources.

### Providers

UNI-verse pulls manga from multiple sources — MangaDex, Asura Scans, Webtoons, Manhwa18, and more. Use the **provider switcher** in the navbar to browse a different source or scope your search.

### Reading a Manga

1. Tap a result to open its **detail page**.
2. Choose a chapter and start reading.
3. Tap **Add to Library** to save it for later (see [Library](#library-and-history)).

## The Reader

The reader is built for long-strip (webtoon) reading.

- **Navigating chapters & pages** — swipe left or right, or use the on-screen arrows.
- **Chapter selection** — open the chapter list to jump between chapters.
- **Reader settings** — adjust layout and reading options. Changes can be applied to just this manga or all manga.
- **Reading history** — your position saves automatically, so you can pick up where you left off on any device.

### Page Comments

Leave comments at a specific spot on a page — perfect for theories, reactions, or joining the conversation in real time.

1. **Long-press** (press and hold) any page to pin a comment to that exact spot.
2. Write your comment and press **Send**. The page shows a marker for every comment and reply.
3. Tap a marker to view the full thread — reply inline or react to the moment.
4. Delete your own comments, or **report** spam and harassment so moderators can review them.

### Page Load Issues

If a page fails to load, tap **Retry** to reload it. Persistent issues usually mean the source is temporarily down — try another provider or check back later.

## Library and History

### Your Library

- **Add manga** from any detail page to save it to your library.
- **Folders** — organize your library into folders (e.g., "Reading Now", "Completed", "Plan to Read").
- **Sharing** — create public links for a folder and share them with anyone. By default your library is private to you.

### Reading History

Chapters you open are tracked in your **History**. Open it anytime to jump straight back into the last thing you were reading. You can clear items or your whole history whenever you like.

## Community

### Posts

Share your thoughts with the community:

- Create a **post** with your message and optional image(s).
- Attach a **folder** from your library to showcase what you're reading.
- Mark adult content with an **NSFW tag** before posting.
- Browse the feed and click any image to view it **fullscreen**.

### Comments and Reactions

- **React** to posts with an emoji of your choice, or reply directly.
- Engage with respect — spam, harassment, and abuse can be reported and removed.

### Reporting

Spot something that breaks the rules? Use **Report** on a post or comment. Reports go to moderators, and the post or comment may be removed if it violates policy. Don't worry — reporting is anonymous.

## Friends and Profiles

- **Add friends** by their username. You'll see friend counts on profiles, and their activity stays a tap away.
- **Your profile** — customize your bio and avatar, and browse your own post history.
- **Badges & themes** — showcase your style with profile themes you unlock in the shop (see [Rewards](#rewards)).

## Rewards

### Coins

You earn **coins** by completing chapters — the more you read, the more you earn.

### Theme Shop

Spend your coins in the **Themes shop** on profile themes, including premium **animated themes** (Aurora, Stardust, Embers, Ocean Waves, Neon Pulse, and Digital Rain). Preview a theme on your profile before you buy, then apply it whenever you like.

### Leaderboard

Compete with the community on the **Leaderboard** — see who posts the most and who reads the most manga.

## Notifications

Tap the **bell** in the navbar to open notifications:

- **Activity** — likes, replies, comments, and friend requests as they happen (real time).
- **What's New** — announcements for new features and improvements.

Marked-as-read items stay read, and your unread badge updates automatically.

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

**A chapter won't open (404 / page not found).**
The source may have changed its links or be temporarily down. Try a different provider, or try again later. If it persists, report the series so we can look into it.

**Pages fail to load or the reader is stuck.**
Tap **Retry** on the failed page, or refresh the reader. Clearing your browser cache can help after app updates.

**I completed a chapter but didn't get coins.**
Coins are awarded per completed chapter. Make sure you finish the chapter to the last page. If you're still missing coins, refresh and check your balance again — if it's still wrong, let us know.

**A comment I made is gone.**
Comments can be removed if they violate our [Community standards](/legal/terms). Your own comments are always editable or deletable from the comment thread.

**A post I didn't make was reported.**
Reporting is anonymous and only reviewed for rule violations. If you believe it was a mistake, you can reach out through the platform.

**Something is broken or missing.**
Check [What's New](#notifications) for known announcements, and consult the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for platform rules.
